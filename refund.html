Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want learners to have clear information before purchasing a course.

Course Purchases

Because digital course access may be provided immediately after payment, refund eligibility depends on whether course access or downloadable materials have been delivered.

Requesting a Refund

For a refund or cancellation request, contact Up Skill Civil Engineer with your name, transaction reference and reason for the request. Each request will be reviewed according to the product terms applicable at the time of purchase.

Payment Issues

If money has been debited but the payment status is unsuccessful, please contact us with the transaction details so we can check the payment status.
