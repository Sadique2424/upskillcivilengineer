PRACTICAL TOOLS

BBS Calculator + Costing & Estimation

Learn the complete quantity-to-cost workflow used in construction estimation.

🧮 BBS Calculator

Prepare a practical Bar Bending Schedule and calculate reinforcement quantities.

  • Bar mark and member identification
  • Bar diameter and number of bars
  • Spacing and cutting length
  • Total bar length
  • Unit weight and total steel weight
  • Bar-wise and total reinforcement quantity
Practical output: Take reinforcement information from drawings and convert it into a usable BBS quantity format.

🏗️ Costing & Estimation

This covers the complete format, not just filling a quantity.

  • Item description, unit and quantity
  • Rate for every work item
  • Amount = Quantity × Rate
  • Concrete: cement + sand + aggregate quantity
  • Brickwork: cement + sand + brick quantity
  • Plaster: cement + sand quantity
  • Steel: reinforcement quantity and rate
  • Material-wise total quantity and costing
  • Grand total of the estimate
Complete format: Quantity → Material Breakup → Rate → Amount → Total Cost. Rates can be entered or updated according to the applicable project/market rate.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About the Course

Do I need previous MS Project knowledge?

No. The course is structured to take learners from the fundamentals toward practical project planning.

Is this course useful for Site Engineers?

Yes. Site Engineers who want to understand project planning, scheduling and progress monitoring can use the course to build these skills.

Is the course only about MS Project?

MS Project is a major part of the course, but the learning also focuses on construction scheduling, baseline management, progress updating, S-Curve, reporting and Planning Engineer workflows.

Will I learn how to create a construction schedule?

Yes. The course covers the workflow of organizing construction activities, setting durations, establishing relationships and developing a project schedule.

Will I learn about baseline and actual progress?

Yes. Baseline management and planned-versus-actual progress are important parts of the course.

Is this suitable for civil engineering students?

Yes. Students can use the course to gain practical exposure to MS Project and construction planning concepts before entering the industry.

Will I learn BBS Calculator and Costing & Estimation?

Yes. The course includes practical BBS calculation and a complete costing and estimation workflow covering quantity extraction, material-wise quantities, item-wise rates, amount calculation and total costing. Examples include cement, sand and aggregate for concrete, and cement, sand and bricks for brickwork.

Will this course guarantee a Planning Engineer job?

No course can guarantee employment. The purpose of this training is to help you develop practical planning skills and prepare more effectively for planning-related roles and interviews.
